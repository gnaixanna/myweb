I like the fact that my last name starts with X, and although my father said exactly the same thing on his website, this is my original thought. It makes me feel like an unkown variable. As for the English name, I was about to be named by my parents as Athena when I was three, but I told them I would not be able to spell that name. So I named myself Anna (well, partly or mostly because of the movie Frozen I.)
